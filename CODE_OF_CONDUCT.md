# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the code, never the person.

## What's expected

- Welcoming, patient language — especially with people newer than you.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Respect that the maintainers' time is donated.

## What isn't tolerated

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexual language, publishing
others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report anything that crosses the line to **swarnilsinghaicse@gmail.com**.
Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code
or contributors that violate this document, and will explain why when they do.
