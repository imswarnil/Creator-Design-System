Before you write

Four things that come up most, answered here so a reply is not the fastest way to find out.

Do you take freelance work?
Occasionally, and only design-system work — tokens, component libraries, and migrating a site onto one.
Can I use this design system commercially?
Yes. It is MIT-licensed, including for client work, with no attribution required.
Will you review my CSS?
If it is short and the question is specific, usually yes. A whole repository, usually no.
